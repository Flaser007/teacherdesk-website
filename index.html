Three Integrated Modules

One calm desk from accommodation plan to reteach plan

Built for real classroom schedules—where percent extensions, school holidays, attendance flags, and Canvas quizzes all intersect.

Accommodations Monitor

Computes exact individualized deadlines and monitors Canvas submissions for compliance gaps before grades finalize.

  • ✓True unit math: percent of work window, class periods, instructional or calendar days, hours, and project-specific rules.
  • ✓Bell-schedule & holiday aware: respects section due dates, no-school days, and existing Canvas overrides.
  • ✓IEP & 504 meeting logs: tracks Offered / Used / Declined supports with per-student CSV export.

Human-in-the-Loop Grading

Prepares draft answer keys, scores, and Canvas fixes while keeping teachers in absolute control of every update.

  • ✓Pre-grading hard-stop lists: flags open extension windows before a grading batch starts.
  • ✓Rendered-image zero guard: blocks zero or blank deductions until confirmed on the rendered page image.
  • ✓Attendance-aware policy: surfaces excused absences vs. uncleared flags with one-click teacher overrides.

Learning Gaps

Turns post-grading item scores into clear reteach priorities without misreading extended-time blanks as mastery gaps.

  • ✓Ranked skill breakdown: surfaces worst-missed questions and skills per assignment or whole course.
  • ✓Time-issue separation: flags blanks from students with extra-time accommodations separately from conceptual misses.
  • ✓Unpublished Canvas drafts: stages optional mini-lessons as unpublished pages until you review and publish.
Compliance Rules Engine

Seven built-in checks (V1–V6 & Hold) so nothing slips through

Select any rule code below to inspect what triggers it and the exact Canvas fix TeacherDesk prepares for your approval.

Violation V1

Late deduction inside extended window

Triggered when Canvas applies a late point penalty (points_deducted > 0) even though the student submitted on or before their individualized extended due date.

Why it matters: Automated Canvas late policies penalize the base class due date unless a matching student override is already in place.

Prepared Teacher Action
Remove late deduction & clear late penalty status
Queues a neutral status update (extended) and restores penalized points—never writing to Canvas until you click Approve.
Closed-Loop Workflow

Four predictable steps—you hold the final key

Designed around a strict teacher-approval boundary so automation handles tedious verification while educators make every judgment call.

1

Read-only Canvas check

TeacherDesk pulls current assignments, due-date overrides, quiz moderations, and submission states without modifying a single score.

2

Compute & flag

The deterministic rules engine recalculates every student's window, catches V1–V6 flags, and puts ambiguous rules on Hold.

3

Teacher reviews & approves

Inspect side-by-side diffs, confirm zeros on rendered pages, edit neutral feedback, and approve or reject each item.

4

Apply & confirm

Only approved updates go out to Canvas, and the next read-only check confirms each fix landed cleanly.

FERPA-First Architecture

Built so student privacy is the default, not an add-on

From display keys to feedback linting and domain isolation, TeacherDesk protects sensitive student records at every layer.

Privacy Keys & Screen-Share Blur

Shows hashed student keys (STU-xxx) by default, with a one-click blur toggle when presenting or sharing your screen.

Neutral Feedback Linter

Automatically blocks comments containing IEP, 504, disability, or accommodation terms from ever reaching student-facing Canvas feedback.

PIN-Gated App Subdomain

Your live workspace runs separately at ecr.teacherdesk.org behind PIN authentication, HTTP-only cookies, and no-store caching headers.

Non-Plan Students Filtered Out

Accommodations monitoring drops rows for students without active plans during import and uses counts-only status digests.
